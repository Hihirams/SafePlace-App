The colour follows your mood.

In SafePlace the accent is not a fixed brand colour: it comes from how you feel. Pick a mood and watch the whole page change, the same way the app does.

How are you feeling?

Right now the app looks bright. That is how the tint works: one accent, and everything else stays paper.

What fits in a SafePlace.

It is where you keep what serves you: a line you liked, the name of a place you want to go back to, a song for the car.

Habits What you want to repeat. The streak counts the days in a row without punishing the ones you missed. streak and record
People Who you want to talk to, what they told you, when it was the last time. by category
Places Addresses, cafés, routes. The things you do not want to search for again. on your map
Music Records, single tracks, the thing you heard in a shop and could not name. with a link
Self-care What settles your head: a routine, a reminder, a note for the hard day. no guilt

Three screens, one idea.

Sections and lists separated by thin lines. Colour shows up in the detail, never in the background.

Your head, on a map that moves.

Every category is a hub and every note orbits around it. Nodes grow with what you write, and the motion depends on how many notes you have and how you feel.

  • Tap a node to focus it and read the note.
  • Pinch to zoom between 0.6× and 3×, drag to move.
  • You can pause the motion. With Reduce Motion it starts still.

It gets written the moment it happens.

The create button opens a blank canvas, with no boxed-in fields. You write, the app suggests a category, and the colour comes from your mood. Afterwards you can change everything, or nothing.

And if the thought came from Apple Notes, you share it to SafePlace and decide whether to keep it or throw it away.

Try it on your iPhone

And when the day ends, it goes quiet.

You can turn on a daily reminder at the time you choose. If you do not open it, nothing happens: the app does not nag and it does not turn rest into another task.

In bed, with the brightness low, dark mode protects the contrast. The mascot is still there, in the colour of the day you had.

Your notes never leave your iPhone.

No account, no sign-up, no server. The app keeps everything on the device and lets you export it whenever you want.

Installing takes three steps.

SafePlace ships as a build signed by me, not by the App Store. That is why iOS asks for a small detour the first time.

  1. Install LiveContainer or AltStoreThey are the two apps that let you install builds outside the App Store on iOS.
  2. Add the SafePlace feedCopy the address below and paste it as a new source in LiveContainer or AltStore.
  3. Install and updateEvery new push shows up as an update. Your data survives between versions.

What about the App Store?

Not yet. SafePlace is a personal project and today it installs through LiveContainer or AltStore. The day it reaches the App Store, this page will be the first place to say so. You will not find a badge here that means nothing.

Frequently asked.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. There is no account, no cloud and no in-app purchases. You install it and use it.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It goes with the app. That is why Settings includes exporting to JSON: do that before deleting it if you want to keep your history.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. The app adapts the reading width and uses three or four column grids on larger screens.

How do Apple Notes get in?

You open the note, tap Share, choose SafePlace, and the note shows up in the Notes tab so you can decide whether to keep it. iOS does not allow reading Notes directly, so the share sheet is the way in.

Is there a dark mode?

Light, dark and automatic. In dark mode the paper turns black and the accent stays, because the colour keeps doing the same job.

Why is it not on the App Store?

Because it is a personal project published build by build. Shipping through LiveContainer lets it update without waiting on a review.
